How to use the atlas

Start with a question, state or industry. Every chart is based on published ASI observations. Missing values are not treated as zeros. Monetary series are nominal. National broad-industry totals are calculated from reported NIC2 categories where a grand-total series is absent, and may be partial. State boundaries can change over time.

For comparison of different units, choose Same starting point (100). For comparing levels, use matching units. National industry data are available at NIC 2, 3 and 4 digits; state-by-industry data are NIC 2-digit only.
